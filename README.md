# 2. Campaign Topic

## Digital Check-in / Responsible Digital Information Awareness

### Campaign Name

**Travel Smart at the Airport**

### Campaign Objective

To encourage passengers to prepare before travelling and verify flight and travel information through official airline and airport channels.

### Target Audience

Domestic airline passengers, particularly passengers who use digital devices to access flight and airport information.

---

# 3. Verified Campaign Messages

The campaign communicates the following verified messages:

1. Check flight status using official airline channels.
2. Keep travel documents accessible.
3. Follow verified baggage and security instructions.
4. Monitor airport display screens for updates.
5. Do not rely on unverified social-media information.

---

# 4. Campaign Restrictions

The campaign does not:

- Use real airline or airport logos.
- Invent baggage limits.
- Invent security rules.
- Include real passenger data.
- Promise shorter queues.
- Guarantee on-time flights.
- Use fake statistics.
- Present an AI-generated scene as a photograph of a real event.

These restrictions help prevent unsupported aviation communication.

# 6. Campaign Design Rules

The following design rules were applied:

- Use dark blue as the dominant brand colour.
- Use sky blue for supporting areas and backgrounds.
- Use warm yellow only for emphasis.
- Use Montserrat Bold for headings.
- Use Open Sans for body text.
- Keep layouts spacious and uncluttered.
- Use only fictional AeroVista Airports branding.
- Do not reproduce real airline or airport logos.
- Do not include unverified operational information.
- Use wording such as "Check official information before travelling" rather than making guarantees.

# 7. Canva Magic Media

## Recommended Generation Prompt

> Create a clean, professional campaign visual for a fictional airport organisation called AeroVista Airports. Show diverse adult domestic passengers preparing for travel inside a modern international-style airport terminal. Some passengers are checking a generic digital flight-information display while others are keeping their travel documents accessible. Use a dark-blue, sky-blue and warm-yellow visual palette with bright natural lighting. Create a realistic commercial-photography style with natural human expressions and proportions. Keep the airport environment plausible and uncluttered. Do not include real airline or airport logos, airport names, readable personal information, baggage limits, security rules, fake statistics, promotional claims, or readable operational flight information. Do not portray the scene as a photograph of a specific real event.

# 23. Student Reflection

## 1. What is the purpose of a brand guide?

A brand guide helps maintain a consistent identity across different designs.

It defines colours, fonts, visual style and tone so that all campaign materials look like they belong to the same organisation.

## 2. How did Magic Media support the campaign?

Magic Media helped create a realistic airport scene showing passengers preparing for travel and checking flight information.

It provided the main visual that could then be adapted into different campaign formats.

## 3. What problems appeared after expanding the image?

The expanded design needed to be checked for changes in passenger appearance, baggage, airport architecture, lighting and unwanted objects.

The main concern was ensuring that the expanded area remained realistic and consistent with the original image.

## 4. What element did you change using Magic Edit?

The selected element was the suitcase.

The editing instruction was to replace the unrealistic suitcase with a realistic modern carry-on while preserving the passengers, documents, display, terminal, lighting and composition.

The surrounding scene remained preserved.

## 5. Why must AI-generated image text be checked?

AI-generated text can look realistic while containing incorrect or meaningless information.

In an aviation context, passengers could misunderstand such information as genuine flight, airport or safety information, so it must be checked before publication.

## 6. Why should baggage and security rules come from official sources?

Baggage and security requirements can vary by airline, airport and journey.

Using official sources reduces the risk of communicating incorrect requirements to passengers.

## 7. How did you maintain consistency across three formats?

I maintained consistency by using the same campaign name, dark-blue and sky-blue colour palette, yellow accent, clean sans-serif typography, modern airport imagery and professional tone.

## 8. What changes were required after resizing?

The layout had to be adapted to the wider 16:9 format.

The image and text positions were rearranged so that the presentation title remained readable and the airport image had enough space.

## 9. How did peer review improve your campaign?

Pending actual peer review.

I would use feedback to identify readability, consistency, accuracy or composition problems that I may not have noticed myself and then make the required corrections.

## 10. Why must AI-assisted designs be reviewed before publication?

AI can generate visual or textual errors even when the final design looks professional.

Reviewing the design helps identify:

- Inaccurate information
- Unrealistic objects
- Privacy issues
- Misleading claims
- Incorrect aviation details

before the content reaches passengers.
